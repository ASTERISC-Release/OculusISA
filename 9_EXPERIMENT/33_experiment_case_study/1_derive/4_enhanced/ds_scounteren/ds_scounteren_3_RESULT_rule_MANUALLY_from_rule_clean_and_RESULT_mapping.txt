RULE 9: The scounteren register is a 32-bit WARL register; bits that are not implemented are read-only zero, and writes to those bits are ignored.
RULE 12: The scounteren register is readable and writable in S-mode and M-mode.
RULE 13: The writability of a bit in scounteren is independent of the value of the corresponding bit in mcounteren.
RULE 14: U-mode access to a counter is allowed only if both the corresponding bit in scounteren and the corresponding bit in mcounteren are set to 1.
