RULE 1: SFENCE.VMA synchronizes the hart's address-translation caches.
RULE 2: SFENCE.VMA synchronizes the hart's address-translation caches based on the values in registers rs1 and rs2.
RULE 3: Execution of SFENCE.VMA in U-mode raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 4: Execution of SFENCE.VMA in S-mode when mstatus.TVM is 1 raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 5: Execution of SFENCE.VMA in HS-mode when mstatus.TVM is 1 raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 6: Execution of SFENCE.VMA in VS-mode raises a virtual-instruction exception.
RULE 7: Execution of SFENCE.VMA in VU-mode raises a virtual-instruction exception.
RULE 8: Execution of SFENCE.VMA in VS-mode when hstatus.VTVM is 1 raises a virtual-instruction exception.
