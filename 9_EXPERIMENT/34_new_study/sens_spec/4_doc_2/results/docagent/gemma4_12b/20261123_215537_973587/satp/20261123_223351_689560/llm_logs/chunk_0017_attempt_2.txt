RULE 1: The satp CSR is readable in Supervisor-mode.
RULE 2: The satp CSR is writable in Supervisor-mode.
RULE 3: The satp CSR is readable in Machine-mode.
RULE 4: The satp CSR is writable in Machine-mode.
RULE 5: An attempt to access the satp CSR from a privilege level lower than Supervisor-mode results in an illegal-instruction exception.
