RULE 1: The CSRRWI instruction reads the value of the CSR specified in the csr field.
RULE 2: The CSRRWI instruction writes the value of the CSR specified in the csr field into the destination register rd.
RULE 3: The CSRRWI instruction updates the CSR specified in the csr field if the destination register rd is not x0.
RULE 4: The CSRRWI instruction does not update the CSR specified in the csr field if the destination register rd is x0.
RULE 5: The CSRRWI instruction carries a dependency from the CSR to the destination register rd.
