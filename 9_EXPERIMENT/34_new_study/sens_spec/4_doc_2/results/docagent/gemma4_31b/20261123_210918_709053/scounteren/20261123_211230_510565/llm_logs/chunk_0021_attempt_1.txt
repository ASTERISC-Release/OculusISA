RULE 1: When a counter i is delegated (mcounteren[i]=1 and menvcfg.CDE=1), the register state associated with counter i can be read or written via sireg* while siselect holds 0x40+i.
RULE 2: While the privilege mode is M or S and siselect holds a value in the range 0x40-0x5F, an attempt to access sireg* when the counter selected by siselect is not delegated to S-mode (the corresponding bit in mcounteren = 0) raises an illegal-instruction exception.
