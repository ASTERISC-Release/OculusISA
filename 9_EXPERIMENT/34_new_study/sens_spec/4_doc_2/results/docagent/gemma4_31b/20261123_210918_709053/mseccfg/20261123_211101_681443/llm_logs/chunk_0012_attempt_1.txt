RULE 1: The `mseccfg` CSR contains a field named `SSEED` used to control access to the `seed` CSR from S or HS modes.
RULE 2: The `mseccfg` CSR contains a field named `USEED` used to control access to the `seed` CSR from U mode.
RULE 3: If the `[s,u]seed` fields in `mseccfg` are implemented as read-only constant values of 0, writing a 1 to `[s,u]seed` and reading back the result allows software to discover that access to the `seed` CSR cannot be enabled in U and S mode.
