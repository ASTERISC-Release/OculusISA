RULE 1: Upon reset, the mstatus field MIE is reset to 0.
RULE 2: Upon reset, the mstatus field MPRV is reset to 0.
RULE 3: Upon reset, if little-endian memory accesses are supported, the mstatus or mstatush field MBE is reset to 0.
RULE 4: When TSR=1 in mstatus, the SRET instruction raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 5: When TW=1 in mstatus, the WFI instruction may raise an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 6: The operation of WFI is unaffected by the global interrupt bits MIE and SIE in mstatus.
