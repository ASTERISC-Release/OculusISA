RULE 1: If menvcfg.PBMTE is changed, executing an SFENCE.VMA instruction with rs1 = x0 and rs2 = x0 synchronizes address-translation caches with respect to the altered interpretation of page-table entries' PBMT fields.
RULE 2: If menvcfg.ADUE is changed, executing an SFENCE.VMA instruction with rs1 = x0 and rs2 = x0 synchronizes address-translation caches with respect to the altered interpretation of page-table entries' A/D bits.
