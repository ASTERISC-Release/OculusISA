RULE 1: PMP checks are applied to data accesses in M-mode when the mstatus.MPRV bit is set and the mstatus.MPP field contains S or U.
RULE 2: When mnstatus.NMIE is 0, the hart behaves as though mstatus.MPRV were clear, regardless of the current setting of mstatus.MPRV.
RULE 3: If the MNRET instruction changes the privilege mode to a mode less privileged than M, it sets mstatus.MPRV to 0.
