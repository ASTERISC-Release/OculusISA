RULE 1: An attempt to execute SFENCE.VMA in U-mode always raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 2: An attempt to execute SFENCE.VMA in S-mode or HS-mode when mstatus.TVM=1 raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 3: An attempt to execute SFENCE.VMA in VU-mode raises a virtual-instruction exception.
RULE 4: When hstatus.VTVM=1, an attempt to execute SFENCE.VMA in VS-mode raises a virtual-instruction exception.
