RULE 1: A cache-block management instruction's permission to access a cache block is determined by the states of the MPRV, MPV, and MPP bits in mstatus and the SUM and MXR bits in mstatus, sstatus, and vsstatus.
RULE 2: A cache-block zero instruction's permission to access a cache block is determined by the states of the MPRV, MPV, and MPP bits in mstatus and the SUM and MXR bits in mstatus, sstatus, and vsstatus.
