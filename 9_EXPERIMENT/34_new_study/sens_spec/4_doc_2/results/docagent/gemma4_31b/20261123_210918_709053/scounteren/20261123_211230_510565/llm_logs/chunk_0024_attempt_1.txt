RULE 1: In VU-mode, when V=1, an attempt to access a non-high-half counter CSR when the corresponding bit in scounteren is 0 and the same bit in mcounteren is 1 causes a virtual-instruction exception.
RULE 2: In VU-mode, when V=1, if XLEN=32, an attempt to access a high-half counter CSR when the corresponding bit in scounteren is 0 and the same bit in mcounteren is 1 causes a virtual-instruction exception.
