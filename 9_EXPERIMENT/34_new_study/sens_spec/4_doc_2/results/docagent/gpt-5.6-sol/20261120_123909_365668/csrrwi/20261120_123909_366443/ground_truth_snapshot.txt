RULE 1: CSRRWI obtains the XLEN-bit value to write to the CSR by zero-extending the 5-bit unsigned immediate field (uimm[4:0]) encoded in the rs1 field.
RULE 2: If the destination register rd of a CSRRWI instruction is x0, the instruction shall not read the CSR and shall not cause any side effects that could occur on a CSR read.
RULE 3: When rd of a CSRRWI instruction is x0, the instruction shall write the CSR with the zero-extended immediate value.
RULE 4: When rd of a CSRRWI instruction is not x0, the instruction shall read the current value of the CSR, write the CSR with the zero-extended immediate value, and place the original CSR value into rd.
