RULE 1: When the CY, TM, IR, or HPMn bit in the scounteren register is clear and execution is in U-mode, attempts to read the cycle, time, instret, or hpmcountern register cause an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 2: When the CY, TM, IR, or HPMn bit in the scounteren register is set, access to the corresponding counter register is permitted in U-mode.
RULE 3: Any bit in the scounteren register may be read-only zero, causing an exception when reading the corresponding counter while executing in U-mode.
RULE 4: U-mode may access a counter if the corresponding bits in the scounteren register and mcounteren register are both set.
RULE 5: The setting of a bit in mcounteren register does not affect whether the corresponding bit in scounteren register is writable.
