RULE 1: If the Zfinx extension is implemented, the mstatus field FS is hardwired to 0.
RULE 2: If the Zfinx extension is implemented, the mstatus field FS no longer affects the trapping behavior of floating-point instructions or fcsr accesses.
RULE 3: If the Zfinx extension is implemented, the misa bits F, D, and Q are hardwired to 0.
