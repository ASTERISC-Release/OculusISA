# Test Case Rule: RULE 181: If a virtual-machine load/store instruction (HLV, HLVX, or HSV) is used to execute a load or store as though V=1, then vsstatus can affect the behavior of the machine even when the V field in mstatus is 0.
##### ASSEMBLY #####
li t0, 0
csrw mstatus, t0
li t1, 1
csrw vsstatus, t1
li t2, 0x1000
hlv a0, 0(t2)
# Result: a0 != 0

li t1, 1
csrw vsstatus, t1
