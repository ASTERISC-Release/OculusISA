## Chapter 11. "Zicond" Extension for Integer Conditional Operations, Version 1.0.0

The Zicond extension defines two R-type instructions that support branchless conditional operations.

| RV32   | RV64   | Mnemonic                 | Instruction                                     |
|--------|--------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| ✓      | ✓      | czero.eqz rd , rs1 , rs2 | Conditional zero, if condition is equal to zero |
| ✓      | ✓      | czero.nez rd , rs1 , rs2 | Conditional zero, if condition is nonzero       |
