## 32.5.5.4. Zcb Extension

These instructions are compressed versions of I and M instructions that are included in Zkt.

| RV32   | RV64   | Mnemonic   | Instruction                  |
|--------|--------|------------|------------------------------|
| ✓      | ✓      | c.mul      | Multiply, 16-bit encoding    |
| ✓      | ✓      | c.not      | Bitwise not, 16-bit encoding |

| RV32   | RV64   | Mnemonic   | Instruction                       |
|--------|--------|------------|-----------------------------------|
| ✓      | ✓      | c.zext.b   | Zero extend byte, 16-bit encoding |
