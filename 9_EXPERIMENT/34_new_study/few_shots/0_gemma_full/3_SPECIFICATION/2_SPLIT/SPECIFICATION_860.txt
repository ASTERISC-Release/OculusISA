Bits  SGEIP,  VSEIP,  VSTIP,  and  VSSIP  in mip are  aliases  for  the  same  bits  in  hypervisor  CSR hip ,  while SGEIE, VSEIE, VSTIE, and VSSIE in mie are aliases for the same bits in hie .
