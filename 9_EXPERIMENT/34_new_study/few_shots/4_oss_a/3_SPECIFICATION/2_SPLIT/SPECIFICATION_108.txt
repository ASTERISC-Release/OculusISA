## Chapter 21. "F" Extension for Single-Precision FloatingPoint, Version 2.2

This chapter describes the standard instruction-set extension for single-precision floating-point, which is named "F" and adds single-precision floating-point computational instructions compliant with the IEEE 754-2008  arithmetic  standard  [.citation]( ANSI/IEEE  Std  754-2008,  IEEE  Standard  for  Floating-Point Arithmetic , 2008). The F extension depends on the "Zicsr" extension for control and status register access.
