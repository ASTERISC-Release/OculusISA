## 4.4. HINT Instructions

All instructions that are microarchitectural HINTs in RV32I (see Chapter 2) are also HINTs in RV64I.# The additional  computational  instructions  in  RV64I  expand  both  the  standard  and  custom  HINT  encoding spaces.

Table  6  lists  all  RV64I  HINT  code  points.  91%  of  the  HINT  space  is  reserved  for  standard  HINTs.  The remainder of the HINT space is designated for custom HINTs; no standard HINTs will ever be defined in this subspace.

Table 6. RV64I HINT instructions.

| Instruction   | Constraints                              | Code Points   | Purpose                                                                            |
|---------------|------------------------------------------|---------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| LUI           | rd = x0                                  | 2 20          | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| AUIPC         | rd = x0                                  | 2 20          | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ADDI          | rd = x0 , and either rs1 ≠`x0` or imm ≠0 | 2 17 - 1      | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ANDI          | rd = x0                                  | 2 17          | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ORI           | rd = x0                                  | 2 17          | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| XORI          | rd = x0                                  | 2 17          | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ADDIW         | rd = x0                                  | 2 17          | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ADD           | rd = x0 , rs1 ≠`x0`                      | 2 10 - 32     | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ADD           | rd = x0 , rs1 = x0 , rs2 ≠_x2_- x5       | 28            | Designated for future standard use                                                 |
| ADD           | rd = x0 , rs1 = x0 , rs2 = x2 - x5       | 4             | ( rs2 = x2 ) NTL.P1 ( rs2 = x3 ) NTL.PALL ( rs2 = x4 ) NTL.S1 ( rs2 = x5 ) NTL.ALL |
| SLLI          | rd = x0 , rs1 = x0 , shamt =31           | 1             | Semihosting entry marker                                                           |
| SRAI          | rd = x0 , rs1 = x0 , shamt =7            | 1             | Semihosting exit marker                                                            |
