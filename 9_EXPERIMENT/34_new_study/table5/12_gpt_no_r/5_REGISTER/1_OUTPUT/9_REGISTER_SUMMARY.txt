REGISTER: mseccfg
SUMMARY: mseccfg is an M-mode security-configuration CSR controlling lower-privilege entropy-source access, enhanced PMP policy, M-mode pointer masking, and M-mode landing-pad enforcement, with fields determined by implemented extensions. M-mode may read its architectural value and update writable fields at CSR 0x747, while lower-privilege accesses raise illegal-instruction exceptions; if no extension defines any field, the CSR is not implemented. SSEED and USEED permit read-write seed CSR accesses from S/HS-mode and U-mode respectively, while disabled access and read-only seed operations are illegal; M-mode always has read-write seed access. Its PMP fields provide lock bypass, unmatched-access denial, and M-only, S/U-only, or shared-region policies, while PMM selects pointer masking and MLPE enables M-mode Zicfilp enforcement.

REGISTER: mstatus
SUMMARY: mstatus is the machine-level status and control CSR, holding interrupt enables, trap-return state, previous privilege and virtualization state, and controls for effective XLEN, memory privilege, translation, access permissions, and data endianness. Its FS, VS, and XS fields track floating-point, vector, and other extension state as Off, Initial, Clean, or Dirty, with SD summarizing whether any such state is Dirty; other fields support virtualization, landing-pad state, and double-trap handling. Direct access is permitted in M-mode and raises an illegal-instruction exception below M-mode; S-mode instead accesses an exposed subset through sstatus, and WARL fields read back legal implementation-supported values. Notable exceptions include use of an Off extension, restricted supervisor translation operations when TVM is set, SRET in S-mode when TSR is set, and bounded-time WFI or WRS.NTO outside M-mode when TW is set; MPRV affects explicit data accesses but not instruction fetches.

REGISTER: satp
SUMMARY: satp is the supervisor address-translation and protection CSR at address 0x180, selecting the translation mode and root page table for the current address space. Its principal WARL fields are MODE, ASID, and root-page-table PPN; if MODE is read-only zero, translation is permanently Bare and SUM is read-only zero. M-mode and normally S/HS-mode may access satp, while lower-privilege access raises an illegal-instruction exception; S-mode access with mstatus.TVM=1 also raises an illegal-instruction exception, and VS-mode access with hstatus.VTVM=1 raises a virtual-instruction exception. Writes atomically update ASID and PPN, but a write containing an unsupported MODE is ignored in its entirety.

REGISTER: scounteren
SUMMARY: scounteren controls U-mode access to the cycle, time, instret, and hardware performance-monitoring counters through the CY, TM, IR, and HPMn fields. S-mode and M-mode may read and write the register, while any direct U-mode access raises an illegal-instruction exception. U-mode may read a counter only when the corresponding bits in both scounteren and mcounteren are set; a cleared or read-only-zero scounteren bit causes an illegal-instruction exception. When V=1, accesses use the same scounteren state rather than a separate VS register, and a disabled otherwise HS-qualified instruction raises an illegal-instruction exception rather than a virtual-instruction exception.
