REGISTER: mseccfg
SUMMARY: mseccfg is a machine-mode read/write security-feature control register that groups seed-CSR enable controls, PMP controls (RLB, MMWP, MML), PMM, and M-mode Zicfilp control (MLPE), and its implemented fields must read back the architectural value. The seed controls determine whether U/S/HS/VS/VU seed CSR accesses are enabled; disabled modes and disallowed access types raise illegal-instruction exceptions. RLB permits modifying locked PMP rules, MMWP makes unmatched M-mode regions deny by default, and MML switches PMP entries among M-only, S/U-only, shared-region, locking, and execute/access semantics, with disallowed PMP accesses causing access exceptions. PMM writes cannot leave PMM=01 and PMM is read-only zero without Smmpm, while MLPE enables M-mode Zicfilp landing-pad checking so non-lpad indirect targets raise software-check exceptions when set, and when clear M-mode landing-pad checks are disabled and LPAD acts as a no-op.

REGISTER: mstatus
SUMMARY: mstatus is an MRW machine-level status/control register that records trap, privilege-return, interrupt-enable, virtualization, memory-access, endianness, and extension-state status for the hart. Its fields group into saved privilege and trap state (xIE/xPIE/xPP, MPP/MPV, MPELP/SPELP), execution controls (MPRV, TVM, TSR, TW, MDT/MIE, MBE/SBE/UBE, SUM/MXR), and extension-status fields (FS/VS/XS, with SD summarizing dirty state). Extension status Off blocks instructions or CSR accesses that use the corresponding floating-point, vector, or extra state, while state-changing operations update Initial/Clean/Dirty status and Dirty sets SD. Privilege restrictions and illegal-instruction traps apply to vector/FP use when status is Off, to SRET, SFENCE/SINVAL/HFENCE/HINVAL, WFI, and WRS.NTO under the relevant TVM/TSR/TW controls, and traps/returns update saved privilege, interrupt, virtualization, and landing-pad expectation state while returns may also clear MPRV and restore ELP.

REGISTER: satp
SUMMARY: satp is a supervisor read/write CSR that stores the root page table physical page number along with MODE and ASID fields. In S-mode, satp reads and writes are permitted when mstatus.TVM=0, while mstatus.TVM=1 makes such accesses raise an illegal-instruction exception. Writes with an unsupported MODE are ignored without changing fields, and a read-only-zero MODE is always Bare with SUM also read-only zero. If satp is not implemented as an SXLEN-bit read/write CSR, access raises an illegal-instruction exception; otherwise writable bits are reflected on readback, and reserved SXLEN=64 MODE encodings do not select custom translation modes.

REGISTER: scounteren
SUMMARY: scounteren is an SRW register that controls U-mode access to the cycle, time, instret, and hpmcountern counters through corresponding CY, TM, IR, and HPM n bits. When a control bit is clear, a U-mode read of the corresponding counter causes an illegal-instruction exception; when set, access is permitted. U-mode access requires the corresponding bits in both scounteren and mcounteren to be set, and an mcounteren bit does not affect whether the corresponding scounteren bit is writable. A scounteren bit that is read-only zero means U-mode reads of the corresponding counter will cause an exception.
