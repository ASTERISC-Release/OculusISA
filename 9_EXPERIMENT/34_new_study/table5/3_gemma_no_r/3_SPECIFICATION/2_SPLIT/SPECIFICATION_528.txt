## 32.4.3. Access Control to seed

The Zkr extension adds the SSEED and USEED fields to the mseccfg CSR to control access to the seed CSR from U, S, or HS modes (see Privileged ISA specification).

Systems  should  implement  carefully  considered  access  control  policies  from  lower  privilege  modes  to physical entropy sources. The system can trap attempted access to seed and feed a less privileged client virtual entropy source data (Section 32.4.2.3) instead of invoking an SP 800-90B (Section 32.4.2.1) or PTG.2 (Section  32.4.2.2) physical  entropy  source .  Emulated seed data  generation  is  made  with  an  appropriately seeded, secure software DRBG. See Section 32.7.3.5 for security considerations related to direct access to entropy sources.

Implementations may implement mseccfg such that [s,u]seed is  a  read-only constant value 0 .  Software may discover if access to the seed CSR can be enabled in U and S mode by writing a 1 to [s,u]seed and reading back the result.
