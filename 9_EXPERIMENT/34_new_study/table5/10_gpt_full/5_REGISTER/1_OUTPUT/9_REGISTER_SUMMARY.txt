REGISTER: mseccfg
SUMMARY: mseccfg is an M-mode security-configuration CSR controlling seed-CSR availability, enhanced PMP behavior, pointer masking, and M-mode landing-pad enforcement. mseccfg is readable and writable in M-mode subject to field-specific constraints, while any S-mode or U-mode access raises an illegal-instruction exception; SSEED and USEED permit only read-write seed accesses in lower modes, with M-mode seed access always available. RLB controls modification of locked PMP entries, MMWP changes the unmatched M-mode PMP policy to deny, and sticky MML enables mode-specific and Shared-Region PMP semantics; PMM selects pointer masking when implemented, and MLPE enables M-mode landing-pad enforcement. Disallowed seed accesses raise illegal-instruction exceptions, PMP permission violations raise the corresponding access-fault exception, and MLPE-enabled landing-pad violations raise software-check exceptions.

REGISTER: mstatus
SUMMARY: mstatus is the machine-level read/write status CSR controlling privilege, interrupts, trap return, memory-access behavior, virtualization, and extension state; direct access is permitted in M-mode, while S-mode or U-mode access raises an illegal-instruction exception. Trap entry saves prior privilege and interrupt-enable state and disables the target mode's interrupts, while xRET restores privilege and interrupt state and clears MPRV when returning below M-mode. FS, VS, and XS encode extension state as Off, Initial, Clean, or Dirty, with SD summarizing dirty state; accessing disabled state traps, while modifying enabled state generally marks it Dirty, including both machine and virtual-supervisor state where applicable. MPRV/MPP, endian, and XLEN fields govern effective data-access privilege, byte order, and lower-mode width, while TVM, TSR, and TW can cause specified translation operations, SRET, WFI, or WRS.NTO outside M-mode to raise illegal-instruction exceptions.

REGISTER: satp
SUMMARY: satp selects supervisor address-translation state, combining the translation MODE, address-space identifier (ASID), and root page-table physical page number (PPN). It is an SXLEN-bit CSR with implementation-defined WARL behavior for PPN and ASID; unsupported MODE writes are ignored entirely, and a read-only-zero MODE remains Bare. M-mode and S/HS-mode may access satp, but S-mode reads or writes raise an illegal-instruction exception when mstatus.TVM=1, and all U-mode accesses raise an illegal-instruction exception.

REGISTER: scounteren
SUMMARY: scounteren controls U-mode access to the cycle, time, instret, and hardware performance-monitor counters through the CY, TM, IR, and corresponding HPMn fields. A U-mode counter read is permitted only when the corresponding bits in both scounteren and mcounteren are set; otherwise it raises an illegal-instruction exception, and a read-only-zero scounteren bit indicates such access will fail. The scounteren CSR is implemented and readable and writable from S/HS-mode and M-mode without exception, including when the H extension is active, whereas any U-mode access to scounteren itself raises an illegal-instruction exception. Setting a bit in mcounteren does not affect whether the corresponding scounteren bit is writable.
