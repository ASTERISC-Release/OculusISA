RULE 42: mseccfg.MML is a sticky bit, meaning once set it cannot be unset until a PMP reset.

RULE 13: When mstatus.TVM=1, an attempt to read the satp CSR while executing in S-mode raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 14: When mstatus.TVM=1, an attempt to write the satp CSR while executing in S-mode raises an illegal-instruction exception.
RULE 15: When mstatus.TVM=0, reading the satp CSR is permitted in S-mode.

RULE 18: The mstatus.TVM mechanism supports intercepting supervisor virtual-memory management operations, including satp CSR accesses, to improve virtualization efficiency.
RULE 19: Trapping satp accesses, together with SFENCE.VMA and SINVAL.VMA instructions, supports virtualization by permitting guest operating systems to execute in S-mode while allowing interception of those operations.
