CSRRWI atomically reads-modifies-writes a single CSR, whose CSR specifier is encoded in the 12bit csr field of the instruction held in bits 31-20. It updates the CSR using an XLEN-bit value obtained by zero-extending a 5-bit unsigned immediate (uimm[4:0]) field encoded in the rs1 field.
