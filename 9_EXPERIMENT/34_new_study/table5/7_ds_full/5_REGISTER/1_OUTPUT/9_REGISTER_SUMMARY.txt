REGISTER: mseccfg
SUMMARY: mseccfg is the machine-mode read-write control/status register for security features, grouping seed CSR access enables (USEED/SSEED), PMP/Smepmp policy controls (RLB, MMWP, MML, PMM), and M-mode Zicfilp landing-pad enforcement (MLPE). It is accessible without exception in M-mode, while S-mode and U-mode accesses raise an illegal-instruction exception. USEED and SSEED gate seed CSR access from the corresponding lower privileges: when clear, access raises illegal-instruction; when set, read-write seed access is permitted, other seed access types still raise illegal-instruction, and M-mode seed access is always allowed. MML and MMWP change PMP default and rule behavior, including sticky/locked policy, M-mode-only or S/U-only and shared regions, and related PMP access-fault behavior, while MLPE enables or disables M-mode landing-pad checks and related ELP/LPAD effects.

REGISTER: mstatus
SUMMARY: mstatus is the machine-level read/write status and control register that holds global interrupt-enable and previous-privilege state used by traps and xRET, along with WARL/read-only fields for extension context status (FS/VS/XS and SD), endianness, virtualization/guest state, memory-privilege and access controls, and landing-pad state. M-mode accesses are permitted; direct S-mode or U-mode accesses raise illegal-instruction exceptions, and writes are legalized according to writable-field and WARL rules. Control bits such as TVM, TW, and TSR can make S-mode satp access, fence/invalidate operations, WFI, or SRET illegal under specified conditions, while disabled floating-point or vector context causes illegal-instruction exceptions for the corresponding instructions or CSR accesses. Trap entry and xRET update saved interrupt-enable, previous privilege/virtualization, and related status fields, with MRET/SRET also clearing MPRV when returning to a less-privileged mode and updating MDT/landing-pad state as specified.

REGISTER: satp
SUMMARY: satp is an SXLEN-bit SRW supervisor CSR holding address-translation state, including MODE, ASID, and PPN fields. If MODE is read-only zero, MODE is always Bare and SUM is read-only zero; ASID and PPN are writable fields with implementation-dependent/ASID and WARL/PPN behavior, and a write with an unsupported MODE is ignored entirely. In S-mode, satp reads and writes are permitted when TVM=0 and raise an illegal-instruction exception when TVM=1; U-mode accesses are illegal. M-mode and HS-mode accesses do not raise exceptions, including when the listed extensions are active.

REGISTER: scounteren
SUMMARY: scounteren is a supervisor CSR whose bits enable U-mode access to the cycle, time, instret, and hpmcountern counters. Its CY, TM, IR, and HPMn fields gate the corresponding U-mode counter reads, which are permitted only when the matching scounteren enable bit and any required mcounteren permission allow access. Supervisor and higher privilege modes can read or write scounteren without an exception, including when the H extension is active, and writes update its implemented bits; U-mode access to scounteren itself raises an illegal-instruction exception. If scounteren is not implemented, allowed CSR accesses raise an illegal-instruction exception, while a clear corresponding enable bit or a read-only-zero bit causes the relevant U-mode counter access to raise an illegal-instruction exception.
