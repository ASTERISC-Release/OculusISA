## 35.5.5. c.ld

## Synopsis

## Description

Loads a 64-bit  value  into  registers rd' and rd'+1 .  It  computes  an  effective  address  by  adding  the  zeroextended offset, scaled by 8, to the base address in register rs1'.

Included in: Zclsd
